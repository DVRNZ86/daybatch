# IDEAS.md — parking lot

New ideas land here with a one-line rationale. Nothing exits mid-phase; entries are considered between phases only.

## Pre-seeded (Stage D candidates, premium hard modes)

- **Endless Crossing** — continuous boards until lives exhausted; score = longest run; personal best tracked. Rationale: natural "one more go" premium mode built from existing Crossing logic.
- **Codebreak: Repeats** — symbols may repeat (16,807 codes), 10 guesses; duplicate-letter Wordle verdict rules. Rationale: proven hard-mode formula, big difficulty jump with minimal new UI.

  - Notes: "bite-size" sets time expectation; "same batch for everyone" is the social hook; "a new batch is ready 🌅" matches the in-app rollover banner and B5's planned onboarding line.

- **UI modernisation pass (B5 candidate)** — depth via layered shadows, springy tap micro-interactions, actually load Space Grotesk/IBM Plex Mono (CSS names them but never loads them), dark mode, richer tab chips. Rationale: current look is v13-faithful by contract; a deliberate polish pass fits B5's scope. (Filed 11 Jul 2026 from Darren's feedback.)
- **Crossing difficulty tuning** — on ~13% of seeds (measured over 2,000) the first tap's zero-cascade reveals a safe corridor to the flag, making the round a deduction-free walk; on other days it plays as intended. Levers: cap cascade reach, bias trap layout so the goal row stays hidden, tune the 34% trap density. Changes generation → breaks same-date-same-puzzle, so it needs its own approved phase and a fresh seed contract. (Filed 11 Jul 2026 from Darren's playtest.)
- **Label the two streaks (B5 candidate)** — Darren's first reaction to 🔥2 (header, batch streak: ≥1 game/day) vs "✨ Perfect batch — streak 1" (all five/day) was "what's the difference?" Real users will ask the same. Options: tooltip/tap-hint on the 🔥 chip, or rename the report line to "All-five streak". (Filed 12 Jul 2026 from Darren's phone test.)
- **Crossing Retry erases the daily attempt** — Retry mid-game wipes progress with no trace, and after a finish it clears the result bar so the first attempt can't be re-shared from the board (the history record itself survives — first completion stands — but no UI surfaces it until B5's stats screen). Options: remove Retry for dailies (keep for practice), or lock the first attempt's result as the shareable one and treat retries as unscored replays. Needs a decision on daily-replay semantics. (Filed 11 Jul 2026 from Darren's playtest.)
- **Lexi content depth** — only 222 of the 430 six-letter dictionary words qualify as puzzles (7–16 targets), and the daily hash can repeat one at any time. Two levers: expand the 6-letter dictionary (every qualifying addition = a new puzzle) and/or add no-repeat daily scheduling once history storage exists (B2+). Rationale: ~7 months of unique dailies is thin if the game gets traction. (Filed 11 Jul 2026.)
- **Lexi dictionary gaps + bonus words** — the curated 2,425-word list is US-spelled and has holes (Darren's playtest: "teen" and "centre" rejected; "center" present). Bigger design gap: only the day's *target* words are accepted — any other legitimate word shakes red with no credit. Consider a broader validation dictionary with a "bonus words" counter (Wordscapes convention), separate from the curated target list. Changes accepted-word behaviour → needs a phase + approval. (Filed 11 Jul 2026 from Darren's playtest.) **Promoted to PLAN.md B6, 9 Aug 2026** — both levers (expand curated list + bonus-word layer) approved together.
- **Accessibility pass (B5 candidate)** — B4's Lighthouse run (accessibility category, out of scope for the "PWA green" acceptance bar) surfaced pre-existing gaps inherited from v13's structure: game-grid buttons have no accessible name, several text/background pairs fail contrast, viewport meta sets `user-scalable=no`, and there's no `<main>` landmark. Rationale: fits naturally alongside the already-planned UI modernisation pass rather than a one-off fix mid-B4. (Filed 12 Jul 2026 from the B4 Lighthouse run.)

## Stage D — monetization & go-to-market (spec'd, see PLAN.md A9/D1)

- **Monetization model + marketing plan** — Darren's original brief (before this repo existed) asked for a plan to "implement, market and monetise." Now specified: three-tier pricing ($2/mo, $20/yr, $30 lifetime), redemption-code entitlement, no accounts — see PLAN.md A9 (architecture) and D1 (phase/acceptance criteria). Still outstanding: the actual go-to-market/marketing timeline itself, anchored on the 10 Sep 2026 launch (EPOCH). (Filed 12 Jul 2026, updated 12 Jul 2026 once D1 was spec'd.)
- **Web-only vs. native wrap (Capacitor)** — decided for now: stay web-only (PWA + Stripe, no accounts) deliberately, to build traction before spending on a native wrap. Not permanent — a future Capacitor wrap would unlock native App/Play Store IAP + store discovery, but it's a real architecture shift, out of scope until web traction actually justifies it. Revisit once there's a real signal (retained users, revenue from D1) worth the engineering cost. (Filed 12 Jul 2026, decided 12 Jul 2026, see PLAN.md A9.)
- **Sonar real-time multiplayer ("play another user")** — parked explicitly, contingent on demonstrated traction after D1 ships. Real head-to-head play needs a realtime backend (matchmaking, live state sync) — a fundamentally larger and different build than anything else in D1 (which is entirely client-side + one stateless verify function). Do not build until there's real demand. (Filed 12 Jul 2026 from Darren's D1 scoping.)
- **Endless Crossing redesign — scrolling board instead of discrete boards** — current Endless (shipped in D1) clears one full 5×5 board at a time on shared lives; Darren's phone test found this "pretty tame" and wants continuous generation instead: 2-3 rows pre-generated below the visible area, board scrolls/rolls off the top as you advance, never a full-board reset. Bigger rebuild than a tweak — Crossing's generation is currently a single fixed-size grid (`src/games/crossing.js` `gen()`), not a scrolling/streaming structure. (Filed 20 Jul 2026 from Darren's D1 phone test.)
- **Crossing "Minefield" hard mode** — Darren's idea: a variant with exactly one safe path and every other cell a bomb (vs. the daily's ~34% trap density). Distinct from Endless; could be its own premium mode alongside it. (Filed 20 Jul 2026 from Darren's D1 phone test.)
- **Sonar "more boats" hard mode** — a variant with additional/larger vessels than the daily's fixed 3-ship fleet, raising difficulty. Possible pairing with the hint-tier-penalty fix (see PLAN.md D1 patch notes) so the hard mode has real stakes. (Filed 20 Jul 2026 from Darren's D1 phone test.)
- **Usage analytics/telemetry** — the app is offline-first with no accounts, so there's currently zero visibility into real usage (DAU, which games get played, completion/drop-off, share-card conversion). Darren wants to quantify usage to inform the D2 go-to-market push. Explicitly named in PLAN.md's "Out of Stage B" out-of-scope list, so this needs its own phase decision, not a drop-in add. (Filed 9 Aug 2026 from Darren.) **Promoted to PLAN.md B6, 9 Aug 2026** — GA4 primary (consent-gated) + Cloudflare Web Analytics as an ungated secondary/backup; decided after weighing self-hosted vs. third-party options with Darren.
- **daybatch.com as a marketing site, separate from the daybatch.app game** — Darren owns both domains. Plan direction (9 Aug 2026, reconfirmed 9 Aug 2026 after the technical finding below): `.app` stays exactly as-is (the game, unchanged — zero risk to the existing share-card URL contract or installed PWA links); `.com` becomes a real marketing/landing site with actual content, built as its own phase after B6 ships (not folded in). Notes from the analytics discussion that apply here: an analytics dashboard is always external to the site itself (Cloudflare dash or analytics.google.com), never a page inside daybatch.app/.com; GA4 can track both domains under one property for a unified usage picture, or as separate properties, either is a config choice not an architecture one. Open questions for that future phase: exact content/structure of the `.com` site, hosting approach (still GitHub Pages? same repo or separate?).
  - **Current-state technical finding (9 Aug 2026):** `.com` is not idle/unbuilt today — it's a live, Cloudflare-proxied domain that currently just **redirects to `.app`** (exact mechanism — Redirect Rule vs. Worker route — not yet confirmed; set up in a prior session). `.app` resolves straight to GitHub Pages, not Cloudflare-proxied at all. This is why Cloudflare showed `.com` at ~1.4k unique visitors/30 days but `.app` at 0: Cloudflare only sees traffic touching its own edge, and only `.com` is proxied through it. **A pure redirect serves no HTML/JS, so no analytics tracker (GA4 or Cloudflare Web Analytics, any vendor) can run on `.com` while it's redirect-only** — confirmed with Darren, not worth instrumenting in its current form. Once the marketing-site phase replaces the bare redirect with real content, a tracker becomes possible and should be added directly to `.com` at that point. Until then, the `.com`→`.app` referral signal shows up for free in `.app`'s own GA4 Acquisition reports via the `Referer` header — no separate setup needed.
- **Streak/history transfer on device change** — the premium code transfers entitlement, but streaks/history are device-local by design (no accounts): a customer's new phone starts at streak zero. Two mitigation levers, in ascending effort: (1) local export/import of the storage root — zero backend, natural home beside the code in the Manage view; (2) code-keyed cloud snapshot in Worker KV — possession of the code proves ownership, but this puts personal play data on the server for the first time and needs a two-device merge story. Original Wordle lived with local-only streaks for years, so (1) is likely enough. (Filed 19 Jul 2026 from Darren's D1 device-transfer question.)

## Filed 9 Aug 2026 — from an external GPT review of the live site

Darren ran daybatch.app past GPT for outside feedback. Most of its suggestions were already-built (batch score, brain-variety across the 5 games, free-forever core + premium-as-bonus philosophy, seed reproducibility) — those needed no action, just confirmed the existing design is sound. Genuinely new items, not yet scoped into any phase:

- **Pre-play "Today's Batch · 0/5 complete" header** — a lightweight progress indicator visible before playing anything (currently the Batch Report only appears once ≥1 game is done). Cheap, UI-only.
- **"No account, ever" messaging** — already true architecturally (single localStorage root, zero server dependency for gameplay), just not said out loud anywhere in the app's copy (onboarding banner, help overlay). Cheap, copy-only.
- **Small personality/completion copy** — e.g. a playful line once all 5 are done, beyond the existing "✨ Perfect batch" line. Cheap, UI-only.
- **One or two new permanent games** (6th/7th, not a rotating pool — see rejected idea below) — GPT's strongest concrete suggestions: **Higher/Lower** (trivia-style "which is taller/bigger/older" comparison, different brain-feel from all 5 existing games, easy scoring via a running "N/10 correct" count) and **Reflex** (pure reaction-time tap game, no puzzle-solving at all — a deliberately different category). Both are trivially seed-deterministic (per-day theme/sequence generated from `dailySeed`, same as every existing game). "Game six" is explicitly named in PLAN.md's original "Out of Stage B" exclusion list — deferred, never decided against; this is the natural point to revisit it now that B1–B6/D1 are all shipped.
- **Anonymous premium-only percentile ("you beat X% of premium players today")** — discussed in depth with Darren 9 Aug 2026: reuses the existing redemption-code verification (`verifyCode()`) purely as a submission gate, not an identity — a player's Batch Score (0–100, already computed client-side by `dayScore()`) gets submitted to a small new endpoint on the *existing* entitlement Worker, keyed by a hashed (not raw) code + date for same-day dedupe/upsert, aggregated into a same-day count. No visible leaderboard, no per-user history retained beyond the day, no accounts. Sharing the percentage is the player's choice (not auto-appended to the share card). **Requires Darren's explicit sign-off before building** (same bar as A9's original Worker exception) since it's genuinely new server-side storage, not just reads. **Needs a minimum-sample-size floor** (e.g. don't show a percentage below ~10 same-day submissions) or early low-traffic days will show meaningless/embarrassing numbers like "beat 100% of players." **Darren's call (9 Aug 2026): park for a future update, not this next phase.**

- **Wordle-style colored-square share card** — GPT's suggestion: replace (or offer alongside) the current text-line share format with a colored-emoji-grid per game (🟩🟩🟩 style), matching the "no spoilers, instant curiosity" pattern Wordle popularized. **Not a free tweak** — the current format (`DAYBATCH #14 · 85/100 🔥7\n🧮 Tally — Best path ⛳\n...`) is explicit exact-string contract text, tested across the suite (PLAN.md B3). Changing it is a deliberate decision needing Darren's sign-off on its own merits, not a drive-by UI change — the current text-label format already achieves the same "no spoiler, curious recipient" goal via descriptive lines rather than squares, so this is a real either/or choice, not a strict upgrade.
- **Archive reframed as a "time machine," not a database** — copy-only change: "Previous Batches" list framing instead of a plain date list, plus reassurance copy like "Missed yesterday? You can still play it — your streak won't be affected" near the archive entry point. The underlying behavior already matches this (Archive/History are premium, replay any past date as practice, never touches streak/history) — this is purely about how it's introduced/labeled in the UI, not a functional gap. Cheap, copy-only, pairs naturally with the other small UI-wording items above.

**Explicitly rejected, not filed as a live candidate:** GPT's "8 games total, algorithm picks 5 each day" rotating-pool model. Breaks too much locked-in structure at once for the benefit — the fixed 5-item `GAMES` array, the exact-string share-card contract (which names all 5 specific games), and the Batch Score formula all assume a fixed, always-identical daily lineup. Keep the existing model (same 5 for everyone, every day) and grow the *permanent* lineup slowly (6th, 7th game) instead of introducing daily variability. Also flagged, lower priority than Higher/Lower or Reflex: Sequence, Odd One Out, Gridlock (more overlap with existing logic-game territory).
